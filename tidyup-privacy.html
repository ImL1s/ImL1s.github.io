Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tidyup ("the App") handles your information. Tidyup is a macOS disk space management tool that scans, analyzes, and cleans your files locally on your device.

1. Information Collection

1.1 No Personal Data Collection

Tidyup does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The App operates entirely on your local device. No data is sent to external servers.

1.2 Local Data Only

The App accesses the following data, which remains exclusively on your device:

  • File system metadata: File names, sizes, timestamps, and directory structures within user-authorized directories.
  • Disk usage information: Total and available disk space.
  • App preferences: Your settings and directory access bookmarks, stored in the App's sandboxed UserDefaults.
  • Purchase status: In-app purchase records managed entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework.

2. How We Use Information

All information accessed by the App is used solely to:

  • Scan and display file and disk usage information.
  • Identify cache files, duplicates, and cleanable items.
  • Execute cleanup operations (moving items to Trash).
  • Remember your authorized directory access between sessions.
  • Manage your Pro upgrade status.

3. App Sandbox & Permissions

Tidyup runs within the macOS App Sandbox. The App can only access directories that you explicitly authorize via the system file picker. Your authorization is stored as a security-scoped bookmark and can be revoked at any time through the App's Settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Tidyup uses the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions. Transactions are handled entirely by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck: For privacy-first, anonymous usage analytics. TelemetryDeck does not collect personal data, device identifiers, or IP addresses. It only records aggregated, non-identifiable usage signals (e.g., which features are used) to help us improve the App. See TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

Tidyup does not use any advertising or user-tracking services.

5. Data Storage and Security

All data remains on your device within the App's sandbox. No data is transmitted over the internet. The App uses macOS security-scoped bookmarks to manage file access permissions securely.

6. Data Sharing

We do not collect, and therefore cannot share, any personal information. No data leaves your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Tidyup does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
