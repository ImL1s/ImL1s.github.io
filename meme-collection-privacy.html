Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Meme Collection ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. Meme Collection is an AI-powered meme image management tool that helps you discover, organize, and create meme images from your photo library.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 On-Device Processing

Meme Collection uses on-device AI models to scan and classify images in your photo library. All image analysis is performed locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers for classification purposes.

1.2 Account Information

If you sign in with Google, we receive:

  • Your Google account display name and email address.
  • Authentication tokens managed by Firebase Authentication.

1.3 Usage Data

We may collect non-personally identifiable usage data via Firebase Analytics, including:

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions.
  • Device type, operating system version.
  • Crash reports and performance data (Firebase Crashlytics).

1.4 Advertising Data

To serve ads, we and our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android).
  • General device information and app usage patterns for ad targeting.
  • Approximate location data (country or region level).

On iOS 14.5+, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the IDFA. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings.

1.5 Photo Library Access

The App requests permission to access your device's photo library to scan for meme images. Photos are processed entirely on-device and are not transmitted to any external server.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide on-device meme discovery and classification features.
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts via Firebase.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Send important service notifications.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For serving advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, usage data, and approximate location to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.
  • Firebase: For authentication, analytics, and crash reporting. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription and in-app purchase management. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Tenor/Giphy: For trending GIF and meme content. These services have their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via your device settings.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Children's Privacy

Meme Collection is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
