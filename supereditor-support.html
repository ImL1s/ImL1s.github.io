常見問題

為什麼無法儲存檔案？

確認檔案提供者仍授予寫入權限。若來源只提供暫時或唯讀權限，請使用「另存新檔」選擇可寫入的位置。

文字顯示亂碼怎麼辦？

重新開啟文件並選擇正確編碼。儲存前請保留原檔備份，避免以錯誤編碼覆寫。

什麼是復原草稿？

App 會在私有空間保存加密復原內容，協助在程序中斷或儲存失敗後找回未儲存編輯。

SFTP 顯示主機金鑰變更？

先停止連線，透過管理員或可信管道確認新指紋。不要在無法確認時直接信任新金鑰。

App 會上傳我的文件嗎？

不會上傳給開發者。只有當您主動使用 SFTP 時，內容才會傳到您選擇的伺服器。

如何關閉診斷？

前往設定關閉「可選診斷」。功能預設關閉；關閉後會停止後續收集並清除尚未送出的當機報告。

Frequently asked questions

Why can't I save a file?

Confirm that the file provider still grants write access. If the source is temporary or read-only, use Save As and select a writable destination.

Why does text look garbled?

Reopen the document with the correct encoding. Keep a backup before saving so you do not overwrite the original with the wrong encoding.

What is a recovery draft?

The App keeps encrypted recovery content in private storage to help restore unsaved edits after interruption or a failed save.

SFTP says the host key changed. What now?

Stop and verify the new fingerprint with the server administrator through a trusted channel. Do not trust a replacement you cannot verify.

Does the App upload my documents?

Not to the developer. Content is transmitted only when you deliberately use SFTP, and then only to the server you select.

How do I turn diagnostics off?

Turn off Optional Diagnostics in Settings. It is off by default; opting out stops future collection and deletes unsent crash reports.
