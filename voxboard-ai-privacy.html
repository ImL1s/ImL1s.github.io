Voxboard AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Overview

Voxboard AI ("the App") is a voice-powered keyboard application developed by CB Studio. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data Collection

Voxboard AI is designed with privacy in mind. The App:

  • Does not collect personal data. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Does not store your voice recordings. Voice input is processed in real-time for speech-to-text conversion and is not saved on our servers.
  • Does not track your location.
  • Does not use advertising IDs. The App does not contain ads and does not use Google Advertising ID.

Speech Processing

When you use the voice input feature, your speech is sent to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text for transcription. This data is processed according to Google Cloud's Data Processing terms. The transcribed text may then be processed by Firebase AI (Gemini) for text polishing. These services process your data in real-time and do not retain it.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Cloud Speech-to-Text — for voice recognition
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) — for text polishing
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management (processes only anonymous user IDs)

Data Storage

App preferences and settings are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
