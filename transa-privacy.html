Privacy Policy - Transa

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Introduction

Transa ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our AI translation application.

Information We Collect

Translation Data

Text, voice input, and images you submit for translation are processed in real-time and are NOT stored on our servers. Translations using cloud-based engines (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Google Cloud Translation, Firebase AI) are sent to the respective third-party APIs for processing according to their privacy policies.

Offline Translation

When using offline translation mode with MLKit, all processing occurs locally on your device. No data is transmitted.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) to collect anonymous usage data including screen views, feature usage patterns, and crash reports. This data does not identify you personally and helps us improve the app.

Advertising

The free version of Transa displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash logs and diagnostic data to improve app stability.

Third-Party Services

Transa uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (analytics)
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)
  • OpenAI API (translation)
  • Google Gemini API (translation)
  • Google Cloud Translation API (translation)
  • Firebase AI (translation, Pro feature)
  • Google MLKit (offline translation)

Data Retention

Translation data is processed in real-time and not stored. Analytics and crash data are retained for up to 14 months.

Children's Privacy

Transa is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by posting the new policy on this page.
