About TechDigest

TechDigest is an AI-curated technology news aggregator developed by CB Studio. The app collects and summarizes technology news from publicly available third-party sources such as Hacker News, GitHub, Reddit, and various RSS feeds.

TechDigest does not produce original news content. All articles clearly attribute their original source and author. AI-generated summaries and analysis are clearly labeled within the app.
