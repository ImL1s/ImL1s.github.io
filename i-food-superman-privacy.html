隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 3 月 16 日

1. 概述

食物超人（以下簡稱「本 App」）由 Yushang Lung 開發。本隱私權政策說明本 App 如何收集、使用和保護您的資訊。

2. 資料收集

本 App 不收集任何個人識別資訊。

本 App 不要求使用者註冊帳號或登入。您不需要提供姓名、電子郵件地址或任何個人資料即可使用所有功能。

3. 位置資訊

本 App 使用裝置的定位服務來顯示您附近的便利商店。

  • 位置資訊僅在您的裝置上處理，不會傳送至我們的伺服器
  • 我們不儲存、追蹤或分享您的位置歷史
  • 您可以隨時在系統設定中關閉定位權限

4. 第三方服務

本 App 使用以下第三方服務，這些服務可能會自行收集資料：

  • Google AdMob — 用於展示廣告。AdMob 可能收集裝置識別碼以提供個人化廣告。Google 隱私權政策
  • Firebase Analytics — 用於匿名的應用程式使用分析（如頁面瀏覽次數、功能使用頻率）。不收集個人識別資訊。Firebase 隱私權
  • Firebase Crashlytics — 用於收集匿名的當機報告，幫助我們改善 App 穩定性。
  • RevenueCat — 用於管理應用程式內購買（訂閱）。RevenueCat 隱私權政策

5. 資料來源聲明

本 App 顯示的即期品資訊來自 7-ELEVEN 及全家便利商店的公開系統。本 App 並非由 7-ELEVEN 或全家便利商店開發、經營或授權。實際庫存以門市現場為準。

6. 兒童隱私

本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資訊。

7. 政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將在本頁面公布，並更新「最後更新日期」。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 16, 2026

1. Overview

Food Superman (the "App") is developed by Yushang Lung. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

2. Data Collection

This App does not collect any personally identifiable information.

The App does not require account registration or login. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or any personal data to use all features.

3. Location Information

The App uses your device's location services to show nearby convenience stores.

  • Location data is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers
  • We do not store, track, or share your location history
  • You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect data independently:

  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers to serve personalized ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics — For anonymous app usage analytics (e.g., page views, feature usage frequency). No personally identifiable information is collected. Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — For collecting anonymous crash reports to help improve app stability.
  • RevenueCat — For managing in-app purchases (subscriptions). RevenueCat Privacy Policy

5. Data Source Disclaimer

Near-expiry item information displayed in the App is referenced from publicly available 7-ELEVEN and FamilyMart systems. This App is not developed by or affiliated with 7-ELEVEN or FamilyMart. Actual inventory may vary — please confirm at the store.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
