User Guide

Everything you need to know about setting up and running your automated Bitfinex lending strategies.

1. Connecting Your Bitfinex Account

To allow the bot to automate your lending, you must provide your Bitfinex API keys. We use these keys strictly for lending operations.

Required API Permissions

For your security, you should only enable the minimum required permissions when creating your API key on Bitfinex:

  • Margin Funding: Read & Write (Required to place and cancel lending offers)
  • Wallets: Read (Required to check your available lending balance)

Security Note: Make sure exactly zero withdrawal or trading permissions are enabled. The bot does not need them and cannot access your funds.

2. Understanding Lending Strategies

The Lending Bot supports multiple intelligent strategies designed for different market conditions. You can switch between them at any time in the app.

FRR (Flash Return Rate)

A dynamic rate determined by the average of all fixed-rate funding contracts currently active. The bot lends slightly above or below this rate to ensure maximum utilization of your funds while following the market trend.

Grid Strategy

The Grid Strategy splits your available capital into multiple blocks and places them at staggered interest rates (e.g., 10%, 15%, 20%). If the market interest rate spikes temporarily, your higher-tier blocks will execute, capturing significant yields.

Adaptive AI (Premium)

Our proprietary machine learning model predicts short-term interest rate movements and adjusts your lending rates automatically before market spikes occur.

3. Managing Your Funds

Once the bot is started, it will continuously monitor your Bitfinex funding wallet.

Minimum Lending Amounts

Bitfinex enforces strict minimums for placing lending offers. If your available balance is below this threshold, the bot cannot place an offer:

  • USD: Minimum $50.00
  • USDT: Minimum 50.00 USDT

Auto-Compounding

By default, the Bitfinex Lending Bot fully embraces auto-compounding. As soon as a borrower repays a loan plus interest, the bot immediately detects the newly available balance and includes it in your next lending cycle.

4. Troubleshooting

Why is my active session count 0?

Ensure your API keys are correct and have not expired. Bitfinex API keys may require periodic renewal depending on your security settings.

Why are my funds sitting "Available"?

This usually happens if your requested interest rate is currently much higher than the market rate, or if your available balance is below the minimum required $50 threshold.
