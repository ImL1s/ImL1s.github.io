Privacy Policy

Trap Master: Stop Them All — by Next Level Games

Overview

Next Level Games ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how our mobile game Trap Master: Stop Them All handles your information.

Information We Collect

Our app itself does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. However, we use third-party services that may collect certain data:

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Advertising)

  • We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app.
  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, and general device information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Unity IAP (In-App Purchases)

  • We use Unity's In-App Purchase system to process coin pack purchases.
  • All payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store or access your payment information.

Data Usage

  • Advertising: Device identifiers may be used by AdMob to display relevant advertisements.
  • Analytics: Aggregated, non-personal data may be used to improve game performance and user experience.
  • Game Progress: Your game progress (level, coins) is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy

This game is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us to have it removed.

Data Security

All data transmitted by third-party services (AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

Your Rights

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

隱私權政策

Trap Master: Stop Them All — Next Level Games 開發

概述

Next Level Games（「我們」）尊重您的隱私。本政策說明我們的手機遊戲 Trap Master: Stop Them All 如何處理您的資訊。

我們收集的資訊

我們的應用程式本身不會直接收集您的姓名、電子郵件或位置等個人資訊。但我們使用的第三方服務可能會收集某些資料：

第三方服務

Google AdMob（廣告）

  • 我們使用 Google AdMob 在應用程式中顯示廣告。
  • AdMob 可能會收集裝置識別碼（廣告 ID / IDFA）、IP 位址和一般裝置資訊，以投放個人化或非個人化廣告。
  • 詳情請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。

Unity IAP（應用程式內購買）

  • 我們使用 Unity 的應用程式內購買系統處理金幣包購買。
  • 所有付款處理由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 處理。我們不會儲存或存取您的付款資訊。

資料用途

  • 廣告：裝置識別碼可能被 AdMob 用於顯示相關廣告。
  • 分析：彙整的非個人資料可能用於改善遊戲效能和使用者體驗。
  • 遊戲進度：您的遊戲進度（關卡、金幣）儲存在您的裝置本機，不會傳送至任何伺服器。

兒童隱私

本遊戲適合一般受眾。我們不會故意收集 13 歲以下兒童的個人資訊。如果您認為我們不小心收集了此類資訊，請與我們聯繫。

資料安全

第三方服務（AdMob）傳輸的所有資料均使用業界標準協定（HTTPS/TLS）進行加密傳輸。

您的權利

您可以透過調整裝置設定來選擇退出個人化廣告：

  • iOS：設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤
  • Android：設定 → Google → 廣告 → 選擇退出廣告個人化

政策變更

我們可能會不時更新本隱私權政策。任何變更將反映在本頁面上，並附有更新的生效日期。
