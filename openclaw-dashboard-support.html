Frequently Asked Questions

What is OpenClaw Dashboard?

OpenClaw Dashboard is a companion management application designed to help you interact with and configure your OpenClaw AI Gateway instances and Cloud Relays securely from your mobile device.

How do I connect to my Gateway?

You can connect to your OpenClaw Gateway by scanning a QR code provided by your Cloud Relay or by manually entering your connection details in the app.

Is my AI conversation data stored on your servers?

No. Your interactions with AI agents remain on your selected processing gateway or on your device. We do not aggregate or centralize your private AI conversations. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for more details.

What should I do if I can't connect to my Cloud Relay?

Make sure your mobile device has an active internet connection and that your OpenClaw Gateway or Cloud Relay is running and accessible. If issues persist, please contact us at the email above.
