Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: February 17, 2026

MoltX (package: io.moltx.moltx_app) is developed and published by CB Studio. We are committed to ensuring a safe environment for all users, including children. This document outlines our child safety standards in compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards Policy.

Zero Tolerance Policy: MoltX maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. Any content, behavior, or activity that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors is strictly prohibited on our platform.

1. Prohibited Content and Conduct

The following activities and content are strictly prohibited on MoltX:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including AI-generated content
  • Content that sexualizes or exploits minors in any way
  • Grooming behavior or any attempt to solicit minors
  • Sharing, distributing, or promoting content that depicts or encourages harm to children
  • Any content that violates applicable child protection laws and regulations

2. Enforcement Measures

We take the following actions to enforce these standards:

  • Content Moderation: We actively monitor and moderate content posted on the platform to identify and remove any prohibited material.
  • Immediate Removal: Any content found to violate these standards will be immediately removed.
  • Account Suspension: Accounts found to be in violation of these standards will be permanently suspended without notice.
  • Law Enforcement Reporting: Confirmed CSAM and CSAE activity will be reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or relevant law enforcement authorities as required by applicable law.

3. Reporting Mechanism

If you encounter any content or behavior on MoltX that you believe violates these child safety standards, please report it immediately through any of the following channels:

  • Email: nicepromise@gmail.com — Please include "Child Safety Report" in the subject line.
  • In-App Reporting: Use the report feature available within the app to flag inappropriate content.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated with the highest priority.

4. Child Safety Point of Contact

Our designated child safety point of contact for receiving notifications from Google Play regarding child safety matters is:

  • Name: CB Studio — Child Safety Team
  • Email: nicepromise@gmail.com

5. User Feedback

We welcome feedback from our users regarding child safety concerns and the effectiveness of our safety measures. You can provide feedback through:

  • Email: nicepromise@gmail.com
  • Google Play Store reviews

6. Compliance with Applicable Laws

MoltX, developed by CB Studio, complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • The U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provisions relating to children
  • Local child protection laws in jurisdictions where MoltX is available

7. Updates to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
