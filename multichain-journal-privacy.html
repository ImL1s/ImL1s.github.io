Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MultiChain Journal ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and discloses user information. The App is developed and operated by Yushang Lung.

1. Information We Collect

The App stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Wallet addresses (public addresses only, never private keys)
  • API keys (encrypted using device-level secure storage)
  • Transaction records and account information
  • App settings and preferences

All data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server operated by us.

2. Data Storage

  • API keys are encrypted using system-level secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
  • Account information and transaction records are stored in a local database (Hive).
  • We do not create any account system and do not collect personally identifiable information.
  • Biometric data (Face ID / Fingerprint) is managed by the operating system; the App cannot access or store your biometric data.

3. Third-Party API Access

The App connects to the following third-party services to query asset and price information. Your data is used solely for queries, and we do not share your personal information in any way:

  • Binance / Bybit - Query account balances and transaction history (only via API keys you provide)
  • Moralis - Multi-chain asset aggregation queries
  • CoinGecko - Cryptocurrency price information
  • Etherscan / Polygonscan / BscScan - Blockchain transaction history queries

These services only receive the minimum information required for public queries (such as wallet addresses) and do not receive your personal identity information.

4. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not track your usage behavior
  • We do not collect any analytics or telemetry data
  • We do not sell or share your data with any third party
  • We do not store your private keys or seed phrases
  • We do not request unnecessary device permissions

5. How to Delete Your Data

You can delete all data in the App at any time:

  1. Go to the "Settings" page
  2. Select the "Data Management" section
  3. Tap "Clear All Data"

This will permanently delete all locally stored accounts, transaction records, API keys, and app settings. You can also simply uninstall the App to remove all related data.

6. Camera Permission

The App may request camera permission to scan wallet address QR codes. Camera data is not stored or transmitted anywhere.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be communicated through app update notes or by updating this page.
