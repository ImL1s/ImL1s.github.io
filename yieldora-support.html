Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my Bitfinex account?

Go to Account → API Credentials and enter your Bitfinex API key and secret. Make sure to enable only "Margin Funding" permissions for security.

Is my API key safe?

Your API credentials are encrypted and stored securely. Yieldora only requires "Margin Funding" permissions — we cannot access your trading funds or make withdrawals.

What lending strategies are available?

Yieldora offers Grid, Adaptive (ML-powered), and FRR (Flash Return Rate) strategies. Higher subscription tiers unlock more advanced configurations.

How do I manage my subscription?

You can manage your subscription from Account → Subscription in the app, or through your device's app store settings (App Store / Google Play).

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Account → Subscription and tap "Restore Purchases". This will sync your subscription status across devices.
