繁體中文版

重點摘要：SuperEditor 不要求建立帳號、不含廣告，也不會把您的文件內容傳送給開發者。文件主要留在您的裝置，或依您的指示透過 SFTP 傳送至您選擇的伺服器。Firebase 診斷功能預設關閉，只有在您主動同意後才會啟用。

1. 適用範圍

本政策說明 SuperEditor（以下稱「本 App」）如何處理資訊。本政策不適用於您自行選擇的檔案提供者、SFTP 伺服器、網路服務商或其他第三方服務；該等服務受其各自條款與政策規範。

2. 核心編輯功能處理的資料

  • 文件內容與檔案資訊：您開啟或編輯的文字、檔名、URI、編碼與儲存位置，會在裝置上處理。本 App 不會將這些內容傳送給開發者。
  • 復原草稿：為協助在中斷或失敗後復原未儲存內容，本 App 可能在 App 私有空間保存加密草稿；加密金鑰由 Android Keystore 管理。解除安裝 App 會移除這些本機資料。
  • 本機檔案：檔案存取由您透過 Android Storage Access Framework 選擇並授權。本 App 不會掃描未經您選擇的儲存空間。

3. SFTP 連線

若您使用 SFTP，主機、連接埠、使用者名稱與驗證資料會在裝置上處理，並只用於連線至您指定的伺服器。文件內容會透過加密的 SSH/SFTP 連線在您的裝置與該伺服器間傳輸。已信任的主機金鑰會保存在不參與裝置備份的 App 私有空間，以協助偵測伺服器金鑰變更。開發者不營運、不控制也無法存取您選擇的 SFTP 伺服器。

4. 可選的診斷資料

Firebase Analytics 與 Firebase Crashlytics 均預設停用。只有當您在設定中主動開啟「可選診斷」後，Firebase 才可能處理下列資料：

  • 有限且預先允許的 App 互動事件與粗略結果，例如功能類型、成功／失敗、延遲或檔案大小區間；
  • App 版本、裝置型號、作業系統版本、Firebase App Instance／Installation 識別碼；
  • 當機、ANR、堆疊追蹤、技術診斷資訊及相關 Analytics breadcrumbs。

診斷事件的設計不接受文件文字、檔名、URI、本機或遠端路徑、SFTP 主機、使用者名稱、密碼、私鑰或主機金鑰。廣告 ID、廣告個人化訊號與安裝推薦來源收集已停用或由 App 移除。

7. 保存與安全

本機文件與遠端檔案的保存由您選擇的位置及服務決定。復原草稿只為編輯復原而保存，並受 App 私有儲存與 Android Keystore 加密保護。SFTP 使用 SSH 加密傳輸；Firebase 的保存與刪除依 Google/Firebase 的設定及政策辦理。任何安全措施都無法保證零風險，請自行保留重要文件備份並確認遠端主機指紋。

English Version

Summary: SuperEditor does not require an account, contains no advertising, and does not send your document content to the developer. Documents stay on your device or are sent, at your direction, to an SFTP server you choose. Firebase diagnostics are off by default and activate only after you opt in.

1. Scope

This policy explains how SuperEditor (the “App”) handles information. It does not govern a file provider, SFTP server, network provider, or other third party you choose; those services are governed by their own terms and policies.

2. Data processed for core editing

  • Documents and file metadata: text, file names, URIs, encoding, and storage locations you open or edit are processed on your device. The App does not send this content to the developer.
  • Recovery drafts: to recover unsaved work after an interruption or failure, the App may keep encrypted drafts in private app storage. Encryption keys are managed by Android Keystore. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.
  • Local files: access is selected and granted by you through Android's Storage Access Framework. The App does not scan storage you have not selected.

3. SFTP connections

If you use SFTP, the host, port, username, and authentication material are processed on your device and used only to connect to the server you specify. Documents travel between your device and that server over an encrypted SSH/SFTP connection. Trusted host keys are kept in private app storage excluded from device backup so the App can detect host-key changes. The developer does not operate, control, or have access to your chosen SFTP server.

4. Optional diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics are disabled by default. Only after you turn on optional diagnostics in Settings may Firebase process:

  • limited, allowlisted app interaction events and coarse outcomes, such as feature type, success or failure, latency bucket, or file-size bucket;
  • app version, device model, operating-system version, and Firebase app-instance or installation identifiers; and
  • crashes, ANRs, stack traces, technical diagnostics, and related Analytics breadcrumbs.

Diagnostic events are designed not to accept document text, file names, URIs, local or remote paths, SFTP hosts, usernames, passwords, private keys, or host keys. Advertising ID, ad-personalization signals, and install-referrer collection are disabled or removed from the App.

6. Choice, opt-out, and deletion requests

You may turn optional diagnostics off in App Settings at any time. The App will disable future Analytics and Crashlytics collection, reset local Analytics data, and delete unsent Crashlytics reports. This action cannot guarantee automatic deletion of diagnostic data previously transmitted to Firebase.

To ask about or request handling of previously transmitted diagnostic data, email aa22396584@gmail.com with the App version, platform, and approximate date needed to scope the request. Do not send passwords, private keys, or unnecessary document content.

7. Retention and security

Retention of local and remote files is controlled by the storage location and services you choose. Recovery drafts are retained only to support editing recovery and are protected by private app storage and Android Keystore encryption. SFTP uses SSH encryption in transit. Firebase retention and deletion follow Google/Firebase settings and policies. No security measure can guarantee zero risk; keep backups of important documents and verify remote host fingerprints.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose productivity tool, is not directed primarily to children, and does not knowingly ask children to provide personal data.
