Frequently Asked Questions

What does Tidyup do?

Tidyup is a macOS disk cleaner that helps you reclaim storage space by finding and removing junk files, caches, duplicate files, and unused applications safely.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Tidyup only scans directories you grant access to. All processing happens locally on your Mac — no data is ever uploaded to any server. Deleted files are moved to the Trash first so you can recover them if needed.

What is the difference between the Yearly and Lifetime plan?

The Yearly plan ($9.99/year) gives you full access to all Pro features with an auto-renewable annual subscription. The Lifetime plan ($24.99, one-time) unlocks all Pro features permanently with a single purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your Mac, or go to the App Store → Account → Subscriptions. From there you can manage or cancel your Tidyup Pro subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund for any App Store purchase.
