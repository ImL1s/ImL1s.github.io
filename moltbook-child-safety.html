Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Last updated: February 1, 2026

App: Moltbook - Unofficial Client (com.cbstudio.moltbook)

Developer: CB Studio

Our Commitment

CB Studio is committed to providing a safe environment for all users, including minors. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content or behavior on our platform.

Safety Measures

  • This app is an unofficial read-only client for the Moltbook social network and does not host any user-generated content directly.
  • All content displayed within the app is sourced from the Moltbook platform, which maintains its own content moderation policies.
  • The app does not provide direct messaging capabilities to minors.
  • The app does not collect personal information from children under 13.

Content Moderation

  • We rely on Moltbook's platform-level content moderation to filter inappropriate content.
  • Users can report any inappropriate content encountered within the app.
  • Reported content is escalated to the Moltbook platform for review and action.

CSAE Prevention

  • Any content suspected of depicting or promoting child sexual abuse or exploitation will be immediately reported to relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies in investigations related to child safety.
  • We comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws regarding child safety and protection.
