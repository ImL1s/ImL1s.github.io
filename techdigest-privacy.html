Privacy Policy

App Name: TechDigest — AI Tech News

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TechDigest ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. TechDigest is an AI-curated technology news digest that aggregates publicly available tech news and provides structured summaries.

1. Information Collection

1.1 Anonymized Analytics

TechDigest uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymized usage data. This includes:

  • App usage patterns: Screen views, feature usage frequency, and session duration.
  • Device information: Device model, OS version, and screen resolution.
  • Crash reports: Stack traces and device state at time of crash (no personal data).

This data is used solely to improve the App's stability and user experience. It does not include personally identifiable information.

1.2 No Account Required

TechDigest does not require user registration or login. No email addresses, names, or personal identifiers are collected.

1.3 No Location Data

TechDigest does not access your device's GPS or location services.

2. Advertising

TechDigest displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. You can manage your ad preferences through your device settings.

  • On iOS, AdMob respects your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) preferences.
  • On Android, you can opt out of personalized ads via Settings > Google > Ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy for details on AdMob data practices.

3. In-App Purchases

TechDigest offers optional premium features via in-app purchases managed by RevenueCat. All transactions are processed by:

  • Apple StoreKit (iOS) — See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android) — See Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat processes subscription data. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your payment information.

4. Third-Party Content

TechDigest aggregates news from third-party sources including Hacker News, GitHub, Reddit, and various RSS feeds. Links to original articles may take you to third-party websites with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

5. Data Storage and Security

Anonymized analytics data is stored on Firebase servers (Google Cloud Platform) with industry-standard encryption. No personally identifiable data is stored. Cached news articles are stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties except as described in this policy (Firebase Analytics, AdMob, RevenueCat, and platform billing services).

7. Children's Privacy

TechDigest is intended for general audiences. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

8. International Users

TechDigest is available worldwide. By using the App, you consent to the processing of anonymized data as described in this policy, regardless of your country of residence.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
