PDF God Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 9, 2026

1. Introduction

PDF God ("we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered PDF reader application developed by Yushang Lung. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data NOT Collected

We prioritize your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information.

  • Files: Your PDF files and annotations are stored locally on your device or in your personal cloud storage (if enabled). We do not have access to them.
  • Account: Login is used solely for Google Drive authentication to enable synchronization. We do not store your credentials.

2.2 Analytics Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Crash Reports — collected via Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability
  • Performance Diagnostics — app performance metrics via Firebase Analytics

3. Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to enable cloud synchronization:

  • The app uses Google Drive API to store your app data (annotations, reading progress) in a hidden App Data folder on your own Google Drive.
  • This data is private to you and cannot be accessed by other apps or by us.
  • You can delete this data at any time via your Google Drive management settings.

4. AI Features

PDF God uses Google Gemini API to provide AI summarization and Q&A features.

  • When you use these features, relevant text from your document is sent to Google's servers for processing.
  • This data is used solely to generate the response and is handled in accordance with Google's AI Data Policy.
  • We do not store or use your content for any other purpose.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash reports and diagnostics. Firebase Privacy
  • Firebase Analytics — collects anonymized usage statistics. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Gemini API — processes text for AI features. Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and third-party services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS protocols. We do not store personal data on our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

PDF God is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You may:

  • Disable cloud synchronization at any time within app settings
  • Delete your synced data from Google Drive
  • Request deletion of crash report data by contacting us

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
