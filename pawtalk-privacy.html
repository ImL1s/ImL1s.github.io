Privacy Policy for PawTalk

1. Introduction

Welcome to PawTalk. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information Collection and Use

PawTalk uses microphone access to translate your pet's sounds locally or via Google Gemini AI. The audio recordings are processed solely for the purpose of translation and are not permanently stored or shared with third parties for marketing.

We use third-party services including Google AdMob for advertising and Firebase Crashlytics/Analytics for improving app stability. These services may collect Device IDs and Crash Logs automatically.
