Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PawTalk ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. PawTalk is an AI-powered pet sound translator that records pet vocalizations, analyzes mood, and provides calming music for pets.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Audio Data

The App records short audio clips of your pet's vocalizations for real-time AI analysis. These audio clips are:

  • Processed temporarily by Firebase AI (Gemini) for mood analysis.
  • Not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Deleted from the device after analysis is complete.

1.2 Pet Profile Data

You may voluntarily provide:

  • Pet name, type (dog/cat), and breed.
  • Pet profile photo (stored locally on your device).

1.3 Usage Data

We collect non-personally identifiable usage data through Firebase Analytics, including:

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions.
  • Device type and operating system version.
  • Crash reports and performance data (via Firebase Crashlytics).

1.4 Advertising Data

To serve ads, we and our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android).
  • General device information and app usage patterns for ad targeting.
  • Approximate location data (country or region level).

On iOS 14.5+, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the IDFA. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Analyze pet sounds and provide mood translations.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.
  • Serve relevant advertisements.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical issues.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): For serving advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, usage data, and approximate location to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data disclosure.
  • Firebase: For AI analysis, analytics, and crash reporting. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Pet profile data is stored locally on your device. Audio recordings are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent.
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your data by uninstalling the app (all local data is removed).
  • Opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent for tracking at any time via the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Children's Privacy

PawTalk is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
