Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OpenClaw Dashboard ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. OpenClaw Dashboard is a companion management application for OpenClaw AI Gateway ecosystems.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and Gateway Information

When you use the App to connect to a local or remote OpenClaw Gateway or Cloud Relay, we may temporarily store session tokens mapping to your gateway for authentication. We do not store or transmit your API keys or passwords to external centralized servers without your direct action to a Cloud Relay.

1.2 Usage Data

We may collect non-personally identifiable usage data, including:

  • App usage patterns and feature interactions.
  • Device type, operating system version.
  • Crash reports and performance data.

1.3 Content Data

Interactions you have with your AI agents (messages, sessions) remain entirely on your selected processing gateway or your device. We do not aggregate or centralize your private AI conversations.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality.
  • Facilitate secure WebSocket connections to your Cloud Relay.
  • Improve app performance and user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not serve third-party ads. Analytics and crash reporting tools might be used minimally to ensure application stability:

  • Firebase (if enabled): For analytics and crash reporting. See Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your connection credentials and local app settings are stored securely on your device. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your private data with third parties unless required to comply with legal obligations.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of any telemetry data we might have collected about your app usage. Because we do not store your core conversation data on our servers, you retain full control over it via your own OpenClaw Gateway instances.
