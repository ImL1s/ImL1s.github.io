隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 7 月 17 日

本隱私權政策說明「AI 法律助手」（以下簡稱「本應用程式」）如何收集、使用及保護您的資訊。本應用程式是一款利用人工智慧技術提供台灣法律資訊參考的行動應用程式。

重要聲明：本應用程式提供之內容僅供一般法律資訊參考，不構成任何法律意見或律師─客戶關係。

1. 我們收集的資訊

1.1 帳戶資訊

當您使用本應用程式時，我們可能收集：

  • 匿名登入：系統會自動產生一組假名化使用者 ID，並將該 ID 與您的使用紀錄連結；您不必提供姓名或電子郵件。
  • Google 登入：您的 Google 帳戶電子郵件地址及基本個人檔案資訊。
  • Apple 登入：您的 Apple ID 相關資訊（可選擇隱藏電子郵件）。

1.2 對話資料

當您使用法律諮詢功能時，我們會收集：

  • 您輸入的法律問題內容。
  • AI 生成的回覆內容。
  • 對話紀錄（儲存於您的帳戶下）。

這些資料用於提供對話、引用來源、安全防護與紀錄查閱功能，不會用於廣告個人化或訓練公開 AI 模型。

1.3 存證信函草稿資料

當您使用存證信函產生器時，我們會處理：

  • 寄件人及收件人的姓名與地址。
  • 案件類別、事實經過、訴求、金額、日期及履行期限等您輸入的內容。
  • AI 產生的存證信函草稿、請求識別碼、建立時間及與購買額度核對所需的狀態。

上述資料由您直接提供，僅用於產生、儲存及重新開啟您的草稿、防止重複扣款、驗證購買額度及處理申訴。請僅提供完成草稿所必要且您有合法權限提供的資料，並避免輸入身分證字號、完整金融帳號、醫療、性生活、犯罪前科或其他非必要敏感資料。您可選擇不提供，但缺少表單必填資料時將無法產生信函草稿。

1.4 使用量與購買狀態資料

我們會追蹤：

  • 每日提問次數（用於免費方案使用限制）。
  • 每月使用量統計、訂閱狀態、單次購買次數及已使用的信函產生額度。

1.5 廣告相關資料

為了提供廣告服務，我們及廣告合作夥伴可能收集：

  • 廣告識別碼（iOS 的 IDFA、Android 的 AAID）。
  • 一般裝置資訊及應用程式使用模式。
  • 概略位置資料（國家或地區層級）。

在 iOS 14.5 以上版本，我們會透過 Apple 的 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）框架徵求您的同意後才存取 IDFA。您可隨時在裝置的「設定」中變更此項授權。

2. 我們如何使用您的資訊

我們使用收集的資訊來：

  • 提供並維護法律諮詢服務。
  • 處理您的法律問題並生成 AI 回覆或存證信函草稿。
  • 儲存您的對話紀錄與已完成信函草稿以供日後查閱。
  • 管理使用量限制、訂閱狀態、單次購買、額度核對與付款中斷復原。
  • 顯示廣告（免費方案使用者）。
  • 改善應用程式效能及使用體驗。

3. 第三方服務

本應用程式整合以下第三方服務：

  • Firebase Authentication：用於使用者身份驗證。請參閱 Firebase 隱私權政策。
  • Cloud Firestore：用於儲存對話紀錄、已完成的信函草稿、使用量及額度資料。請參閱 Firebase 隱私權政策。
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini)：用於處理法律問題並生成 AI 回覆或存證信函草稿。您提交的對話或信函內容會傳送至 Google 的 AI 服務進行處理。請參閱 Vertex AI 資料治理政策。
  • Google AdMob：用於顯示廣告。AdMob 可能收集裝置識別碼、使用資料及概略位置以提供個人化或非個人化廣告。請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。
  • RevenueCat 與 Google Play：用於管理訂閱、單次應用程式內購買、購買次數與付款狀態。我們不會取得完整付款卡號。請參閱 RevenueCat 隱私權政策及 Google 隱私權政策。

4. AI 生成內容聲明

本應用程式使用 Google Gemini AI 模型來回答您的法律問題。請注意：

  • AI 回覆僅供一般法律資訊參考，不構成正式法律意見。
  • AI 回覆可能包含錯誤或過時的資訊。
  • 不應將 AI 回覆作為訴訟或法律行為的唯一依據。
  • 如需正式法律協助，請諮詢具有律師證書的專業律師。

5. 資料儲存與安全

您的資料會透過加密連線傳送，並儲存在 Google Cloud Platform / Firebase 或暫時儲存在您裝置的安全儲存區。Cloud Functions 主要在台灣區域處理，Vertex AI 內容處理目前使用美國區域；Google、RevenueCat 與其服務供應商亦可能在其營運地區處理資料。我們實施適當的技術和組織措施，防止未經授權的存取、更改或銷毀，但任何網路傳輸方式均無法保證 100% 安全。

5-1. 個資事故通報

依據中華民國《個人資料保護法》及適用規定，若發生可能影響您個人資料安全的事故，我們將：

  • 於知悉後依法定時限內，向主管機關（個人資料保護委員會）提出通報。
  • 以適當方式（電子郵件或應用程式通知）告知受影響的使用者。
  • 說明事故性質、可能影響及我們已採取的應對措施。

若您認為我們對您的個人資料處理有疑慮，您可向個人資料保護委員會（pdpc.gov.tw）提出申訴。

6. 資料分享

我們不會出售您的個人資訊。我們僅在以下情況下分享您的資料：

  • 與上述第三方服務供應商合作以提供服務。
  • 遵守法律要求或回應合法的法律程序。
  • 保護我們的權利、隱私、安全或財產。

7. 資料保留

  • 對話紀錄及已完成的信函草稿會保留至您刪除帳戶為止；完成帳戶刪除後，與帳戶直接關聯的內容會依刪除流程移除或去識別化。
  • 為避免付款中斷造成重複扣款，未完成的信函請求會加密暫存在您的裝置，並於完成、取消或切換帳戶時清除。若仍未清除，應用程式下次進入信函功能並檢查安全儲存區時，會刪除已存放超過 7 日的資料；若您未再使用該功能，資料可能保留至下次檢查或您清除應用程式資料為止。
  • 購買與額度稽核紀錄可能依防詐、退款、帳務及法定保存需要保留必要期間，並採資料最小化方式處理。
  • 使用量資料按月統計並定期清理。

8. 您的權利

您有權：

  • 查詢、閱覽或請求複製您的個人資料。
  • 請求補充、更正、停止蒐集／處理／利用或刪除您的個人資料；法律或帳務要求保留者除外。
  • 透過應用程式要求刪除您的帳戶及相關資料，或使用下方聯絡方式提出請求或申訴。
  • 選擇退出個人化廣告。
  • 在不提供姓名或電子郵件的情況下使用匿名帳戶。

  • AI 模型可能存在偏誤或不完整性，請以批判性思維評估回覆內容。
  • 本服務依人工智慧基本法七大原則（永續發展、人類自主、隱私保護、資安安全、透明可解釋、公平不歧視、問責）設計與運營。

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Legal Assistant ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is a mobile application that provides Taiwan legal information reference using artificial intelligence technology.

Important Disclaimer: The content provided by this App is for general legal information reference only and does not constitute legal advice or an attorney-client relationship.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • Anonymous Login: The system generates a pseudonymous user ID and links it to your usage records; you do not need to provide a name or email address.
  • Google Sign-In: Your Google account email address and basic profile information.
  • Apple Sign-In: Information related to your Apple ID (you may choose to hide your email).

1.2 Conversation Data

When you use the legal consultation feature, we collect:

  • The legal questions you submit.
  • AI-generated responses.
  • Conversation history (stored under your account).

This data is used to provide conversations, source citations, safety controls, and history access. It is not used for personalized advertising or to train public AI models.

1.3 Registered Legal Letter Draft Data

When you use the registered legal letter generator, we process:

  • The sender's and recipient's names and addresses.
  • The case category, facts, requests, amounts, dates, and performance deadline that you enter.
  • The AI-generated draft, request identifier, creation time, and status needed to reconcile purchased generation credits.

You provide this data directly. It is used only to generate, store, and reopen your draft, prevent duplicate charges, verify purchased credits, and handle complaints. Provide only data necessary for the draft that you are legally authorized to provide. Avoid national ID numbers, full financial account numbers, medical data, sexual-life data, criminal records, or other unnecessary sensitive data. You may decline to provide it, but a draft cannot be generated without the required form fields.

1.4 Usage and Purchase Status Data

We track:

  • Daily question count (for free plan usage limits).
  • Monthly usage statistics, subscription status, one-time purchase count, and consumed letter-generation credits.

1.5 Advertising Data

To provide advertising services, we and our advertising partners may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android).
  • General device information and app usage patterns.
  • Approximate location data (country or region level).

On iOS 14.5+, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the IDFA. You can change this at any time in your device's Settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the legal consultation service.
  • Process your legal questions and generate AI responses or registered legal letter drafts.
  • Store conversation history and completed letter drafts for future access.
  • Manage usage limits, subscriptions, one-time purchases, credit reconciliation, and interrupted-payment recovery.
  • Display advertisements (for free plan users).
  • Improve app performance and user experience.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For user authentication. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Cloud Firestore: For storing conversation history, completed letter drafts, usage data, and credit records. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini): For processing legal questions and generating AI responses or registered legal letter drafts. Conversation or letter content you submit is sent to Google's AI service for processing. See Vertex AI Data Governance.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play: For managing subscriptions, one-time in-app purchases, purchase counts, and payment status. We do not receive your full payment-card number. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

This App uses the Google Gemini AI model to answer your legal questions. Please note:

  • AI responses are for general legal information reference only and do not constitute formal legal advice.
  • AI responses may contain errors or outdated information.
  • AI responses should not be used as the sole basis for litigation or legal actions.
  • For formal legal assistance, please consult a licensed professional attorney.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored on Google Cloud Platform / Firebase or temporarily in secure storage on your device. Cloud Functions are primarily processed in a Taiwan region, while Vertex AI content processing currently uses a United States region. Google, RevenueCat, and their service providers may also process data in their operating regions. We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission method is 100% secure.

5-1. Data Breach Notification

In accordance with the Republic of China Personal Data Protection Act and applicable requirements, if an incident occurs that may affect the security of your personal data, we will:

  • Report the incident to the competent authority (Personal Data Protection Commission) within the legally prescribed timeframe upon becoming aware of it.
  • Notify affected users through appropriate channels (email or in-app notification).
  • Explain the nature of the incident, potential impacts, and remedial measures taken.

If you have concerns about how we process your personal data, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission (pdpc.gov.tw).

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share your data in the following circumstances:

  • With the above third-party service providers to deliver our services.
  • To comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful legal processes.
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property.

7. Data Retention

  • Conversation history and completed letter drafts are retained until you delete your account. After account deletion completes, directly associated content is removed or de-identified through the deletion process.
  • To prevent duplicate charges after an interrupted payment, an unfinished letter request is encrypted on your device and cleared on completion, cancellation, or an account change. If it has not already been cleared, the App deletes it after it is more than 7 days old the next time the letter feature checks secure storage. If you do not use that feature again, it may remain until the next check or until you clear the App's data.
  • Purchase and credit-audit records may be retained for the period necessary for fraud prevention, refunds, accounting, and legal obligations, using data minimization.
  • Usage data is aggregated monthly and periodically cleaned up.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Inquire about, review, or request a copy of your personal data.
  • Request supplementation, correction, cessation of collection/processing/use, or deletion, except where retention is legally or financially required.
  • Request account and associated-data deletion in the App, or submit a request or complaint using the contact information below.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising.
  • Use a pseudonymous account without providing a name or email address.

9. AI Transparency

In accordance with the transparency principles of the Artificial Intelligence Basic Act (effective January 14, 2026):

  • This App uses the Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite AI model to generate responses and letter drafts. Users are clearly informed that the content is AI-generated.
  • AI models may contain biases or inaccuracies. Please evaluate responses critically.
  • This service is designed and operated in accordance with the seven principles of the AI Basic Act: sustainable development, human autonomy, privacy protection, information security, transparency and explainability, fairness and non-discrimination, and accountability.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected such information, we will promptly delete it.
