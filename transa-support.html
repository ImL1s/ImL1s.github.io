Q: 如何使用離線翻譯？

前往「設定 → 離線模型管理」，下載您需要的語言模型。下載完成後即可在無網路環境下使用翻譯功能。

Q: 免費版有什麼限制？

免費版每日提供 30 次雲端翻譯，觀看獎勵廣告可獲得額外次數。ML Kit 離線翻譯不受限制。升級 Pro 即可無限使用雲端翻譯。

Q: 如何管理我的訂閱？

訂閱透過 Apple App Store 或 Google Play Store 管理。請前往裝置的「設定 → 訂閱項目」查看或取消訂閱。

目前支援繁體中文、簡體中文、英文、日文、韓文等多種語言。離線翻譯支援 ML Kit 提供的所有語言對。

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I use offline translation?

Go to "Settings → Offline Model Manager" and download the language models you need. Once downloaded, translation works without internet.

Q: What are the free tier limitations?

Free users get 30 cloud translations per day. Watch a rewarded ad to earn extra translations. ML Kit offline translation is unlimited. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited cloud translations.

Q: How do I manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Go to your device's "Settings → Subscriptions" to view or cancel.

Q: What languages are supported?

Currently supports Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, and many more. Offline translation supports all language pairs provided by ML Kit.

Q: How do I delete my account and data?

Send an email to aa22396584@gmail.com with "Account Deletion Request" as the subject. We will process your request within 30 days.
