Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the news come from?

Crisis aggregates publicly available news from trusted sources including Al Jazeera, BBC, Reuters, Yahoo Finance, and GDELT. All content is attributed to its original publisher.

How are threat levels assigned?

Each event is automatically scored using keyword analysis and source credibility. Threat levels range from Info (lowest) to Critical (highest). This is an algorithmic assessment and should be used for informational purposes only.

Why do map borders look different from my country's official maps?

Crisis uses OpenStreetMap data for geographical boundaries. These may differ from official government positions on disputed territories. The App does not endorse any political stance.

Does Crisis track my location?

No. Crisis does not access your device's GPS or location services. The map shows global events, not your location.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings within the App and tap "Restore Purchases." Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

The app is not loading any news.

Please ensure you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists, try force-closing and reopening the App. Contact us if the problem continues.
