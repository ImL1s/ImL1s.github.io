Frequently Asked Questions

What is RentAHuman?

RentAHuman is a marketplace platform that connects people offering their time and skills with those seeking assistance. Whether you need help with tasks, want to learn something new, or just need someone to talk to, RentAHuman helps you find the right person.

How do I create an account?

You can browse available humans without an account. To book services or offer your own skills, you'll need to create an account using your email address or through social sign-in options.

How do bookings work?

Browse available humans, view their profiles and skills, and submit a booking request. Once the service provider accepts, you can communicate through the app to coordinate details.

Is my data secure?

Yes, we take data security seriously. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

How do I remove ads?

You can subscribe to RentAHuman Premium through the app to enjoy an ad-free experience along with other premium features.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account through the Settings menu in the app. If you encounter any issues, please contact us at the email above and we'll assist you.

How do I report inappropriate content or behavior?

If you encounter any inappropriate content or behavior, please report it immediately by emailing us with details. We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly.
