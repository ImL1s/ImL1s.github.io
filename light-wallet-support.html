Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I back up my wallet?
    Go to Settings → Security → Show Recovery Phrase. Write down the 12 or 24 words and store them in a safe offline location.
  • Can I recover my wallet on a new device?
    Yes, use your recovery phrase to restore your wallet on any device by selecting "Restore Wallet" during setup.
  • Which blockchains are supported?
    Light Wallet supports 40+ chains including Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Solana, TON, Cosmos, Polkadot, Tron, and many more.
  • Is Light Wallet free?
    Yes, Light Wallet is free to use. Standard network fees apply for blockchain transactions.
  • How is my wallet secured?
    Private keys are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in your device's secure enclave. The app supports PIN and biometric authentication.

Security Warnings

🚫 We will NEVER ask for your recovery phrase, private keys, or PIN. If someone asks for these, it is a scam.

  • Never share your recovery phrase with anyone
  • Always verify transaction details before confirming
  • Be cautious of phishing websites and fake support channels
  • Only download Light Wallet from official app stores
