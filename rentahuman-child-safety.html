Our Commitment

CB Studio is committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) content or behavior on our platform. This app is intended for users aged 18 and above.

Safety Measures

  • This app is an unofficial third-party client for the RentAHuman marketplace, where AI agents can hire human professionals for tasks.
  • The app is designed for adult users (18+) and is not intended for use by minors.
  • All content displayed within the app is sourced from the RentAHuman platform, which maintains its own content moderation policies.
  • The app does not collect personal information from children under 18.

Content Moderation

  • We rely on RentAHuman's platform-level content moderation to filter inappropriate content.
  • Users can report any inappropriate content encountered within the app.
  • Reported content is escalated to the RentAHuman platform for review and action.

CSAE Prevention

  • Any content suspected of depicting or promoting child sexual abuse or exploitation will be immediately reported to relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies in investigations related to child safety.
  • We comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws regarding child safety and protection.
