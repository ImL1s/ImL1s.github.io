LendLocal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Summary: LendLocal stores your Bitfinex API credentials and lending configuration locally on your device. We use third-party services for ads, analytics, and crash reporting as described below.

1. Overview

LendLocal ("the App", "we", "us") is a local automation tool for Bitfinex lending. This Privacy Policy explains what data we handle and how. Your Bitfinex API credentials and lending configuration are stored exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device:

  • Bitfinex API Key & Secret — stored in the platform's secure credential store (iOS Keychain / Android EncryptedSharedPreferences). Never leaves your device except when making authenticated requests directly to Bitfinex.
  • Lending strategy configuration — rate ranges, min/max amounts, automation settings. Stored in encrypted local database (Hive CE).
  • Historical lending records — offers, executions, and earnings data fetched from your Bitfinex account and cached locally for display.

This data is never uploaded to any server controlled by LendLocal.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

Service Data Collected Purpose
Bitfinex API Trading/lending data via your API key Core lending functionality
Google AdMob Device identifiers, ad interaction data Displaying advertisements
Firebase Analytics App usage events, screen views Understanding app usage patterns
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, device info, stack traces Identifying and fixing bugs
RevenueCat Purchase history, subscription status Managing in-app subscriptions

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Bitfinex Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (AdMob, Firebase)
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No contacts or photos
  • No financial data beyond what you explicitly configure locally

6. Security

Your API credentials are protected by the operating system's secure storage mechanisms:

  • iOS: Keychain Services with kSecAttrAccessibleWhenUnlocked
  • Android: EncryptedSharedPreferences backed by Android Keystore

We recommend enabling device-level biometric authentication for additional security. LendLocal never requests withdrawal permissions — use read-only or funding-only API keys.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify users through an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
