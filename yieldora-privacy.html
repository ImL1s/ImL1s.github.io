Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Summary: Yieldora connects to our cloud platform to manage your Bitfinex lending strategies. We collect your account email for authentication. Your Bitfinex API credentials are encrypted and stored securely on our servers.

1. Overview

Yieldora ("the App", "we", "us") is a cloud-based lending automation platform for Bitfinex. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it. The App connects to our backend platform to execute lending strategies on your behalf.

2. Data We Collect

Data Type Purpose Storage
Email Address Account creation and authentication Encrypted on our servers (PostgreSQL)
Bitfinex API Key & Secret Executing lending operations on your behalf Encrypted at rest (AES-256) on our servers
Lending Configuration Strategy settings, rate ranges, preferences Our servers (associated with your account)
Historical Lending Records Performance tracking and analytics display Our servers (PostgreSQL) + local cache
Subscription Status Managing your subscription tier RevenueCat (third-party)

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

Service Data Collected Purpose
Bitfinex API Trading/lending data via your API key Core lending functionality
Google Sign-In Google account email, profile name Optional single sign-on authentication

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Bitfinex Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy (AdMob, Firebase)
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • API Credentials: Encrypted at rest using AES-256 on our servers
  • Data in Transit: All communication uses TLS/HTTPS
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with short expiration
  • Biometric Lock: Optional Face ID / fingerprint for app access

We recommend using Bitfinex API keys with Funding only permissions and disabling withdrawal access for maximum security.

6. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. Upon deletion, all your data (including encrypted API credentials, lending history, and configuration) will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify users through an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
