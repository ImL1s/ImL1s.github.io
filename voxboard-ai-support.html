FAQ

How do I enable the keyboard?

Go to Settings > General > Keyboard > Keyboards > Add New Keyboard, and select "Voxboard Keyboard". Make sure to enable "Allow Full Access" for voice input functionality.

How do I use voice input?

Open the Voxboard AI app, tap and hold the microphone button to record, then release to process your speech.

Which AI services are supported?

Voxboard AI supports multiple STT and LLM backends including on-device processing (Whisper, LLaMA) and cloud services. Configure your preferred providers in the app settings.
