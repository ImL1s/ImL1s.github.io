PDF God Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026

1. Introduction

PDF God ("we", "us", or "our") is an AI-powered PDF reader application developed by Yushang Lung. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect and Process

PDF God processes the following categories of data. Some collection is automatic when you use the App, while optional features only process data after you choose to use them.

2.1 Device and App Identifiers

To enforce free-tier daily limits and to ensure the free trial can only be claimed once per device, the App reads the Android device identifier (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID). The App sends this identifier over an encrypted connection to our Google Cloud Function, where it is processed to create a pseudonymous quota key.

  • Purpose: fraud and abuse prevention only — specifically, preventing repeated resets of usage allowances by clearing app data or reinstalling.
  • How it is stored: the raw identifier is processed transiently by our Cloud Function and is not written to our database. It is converted into a keyed cryptographic hash (HMAC-SHA256), and only that pseudonymous hash is retained.
  • What is stored with it: only usage counters (number of AI requests and PDF unlocks used today) and the trial start date. No document content, file names, personal information, or advertising identifiers are stored.
  • Not used for advertising: this identifier is not used for advertising, tracking, or profiling. The raw identifier is not retained in our database; the pseudonymous quota key is retained only for quota and trial-abuse prevention.

Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Mobile Ads, ML Kit, and RevenueCat may also process per-installation, advertising, app-set, or similar identifiers as described in their privacy documentation. Google Mobile Ads uses advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete the Android advertising ID in your device settings.

2.2 App Activity, Diagnostics, and Approximate Location

  • App activity: app launches, screen views, taps, ad interactions, and similar usage events may be processed by Firebase Analytics and Google Mobile Ads.
  • Diagnostics: device/app information, performance measurements, SDK events, and error information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads, Firebase, and ML Kit to operate, secure, and improve their services and the App.
  • Approximate location: Google Mobile Ads may derive a general area from an IP address. PDF God does not request precise device location.

2.3 Purchases

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription history, such as product identifiers and transaction status, to provide paid features, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and measure product performance. PDF God does not receive or store your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

2.4 Optional Google Drive Authorization

If you enable Google Drive synchronization, Google Sign-In authenticates your Google account and authorizes the App to access its private App Data folder. PDF God uses the resulting authorization headers only for Drive operations; the App does not read or store your Google profile name, email address, account identifier, or password.

2.5 Files, Documents, and Other User Content

  • Your original PDF files remain on your device unless you independently store them in a cloud provider. PDF God does not upload complete PDF files to our servers.
  • If you enable cloud synchronization, reading progress, document metadata, annotations, and handwriting are stored in the App Data area of your own Google Drive account. See Section 3.
  • If you use an AI feature, the relevant extracted document text, selected text, and your prompt are sent to our Google Cloud processing service and Google's Vertex AI model solely to generate the requested result. See Section 4.

3. Cloud Synchronization

If you choose to enable cloud synchronization:

  • The app uses Google Drive API to store reading progress, document metadata, annotations, and handwriting in a hidden App Data folder on your own Google Drive. PDF file contents are not synced.
  • The App accesses this data on your device under the Google Drive authorization you grant. The developer does not keep a separate server-side copy of the Drive sync file.
  • You can delete this data at any time via your Google Drive management settings.

4. AI Features

PDF God uses Google Vertex AI (Gemini models) to provide AI summarization and Q&A features. These features are optional and the App asks for your consent before sending document text.

  • When you use these features, relevant text from your document is sent to our processing service (Google Cloud Functions), which forwards it to Google's Vertex AI service and returns the response to you. This step exists to enforce usage limits and generate the answer you requested.
  • The App does not intentionally write your AI prompt, document input, or generated output to our own database. Google processes this content under its applicable service terms and retention controls. See Vertex AI data governance.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following service providers and SDKs. Google Mobile Ads also receives data for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • Firebase Analytics, App Check, and Cloud Functions — usage analytics, app integrity, request routing, and backend processing. Firebase Privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads — advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive — optional authentication and cloud synchronization. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Vertex AI — optional processing of document text and prompts for AI features. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google ML Kit — on-device handwriting recognition plus service diagnostics and usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation, subscription entitlement, and purchase analytics. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

6. Data Security

Data transmitted between the App and the services described above is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to our backend resources is protected by Firebase App Check and server-side authorization. We retain only the data needed for the purposes described above and rely on the retention controls of the applicable service providers.

7. Children's Privacy

PDF God is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You may:

  • Disable cloud synchronization at any time within app settings
  • Use the in-app delete flow to remove local data and, when offered, the App's Google Drive sync data
  • Delete synced data through Google Drive management settings
  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID in device settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
