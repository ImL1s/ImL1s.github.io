Privacy Policy for Tidyup: Disk Cleaner

Last Updated: February 20, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the "Tidyup: Disk Cleaner" application for macOS.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Tidyup is designed to run entirely locally on your Mac. The application analyzes your local file system to help you free up disk space and uninstall applications. All file analysis, scanning, and deletion operations occur directly on your device. No data regarding your files, system, usage habits, or personal information is ever transmitted to us or any third parties.

Analytics and Tracking

Tidyup does not use any third-party analytics or tracking frameworks. We do not track your behavior within the app.

Changes to This Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy in the future, we will post those changes on this page. However, our commitment to your privacy and our core principle of not collecting your data will not change.
