Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how "Todo Speak AI" (the "App") collects, uses, stores, and discloses user information. The App is developed and operated by the developer.

1. Types of Information Collected

1.1 Todo Data

The App collects the following information related to your task management:

  • Todo titles, descriptions, and priority levels you create
  • Task completion status and timestamps
  • Original voice input text
  • Subtask and category data

This data is stored locally on your device and in Firebase Cloud Firestore (when cloud sync is enabled).

1.2 Account Information

When you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Account identifier

You may also use the App as a guest without providing any account information.

1.3 Microphone & Speech Recognition

The App requires access to your device's microphone and speech recognition capabilities to:

  • Convert speech to text for creating todo items

Voice data is processed only when you actively use the voice input feature. We do not permanently store raw audio recordings.

1.4 AI Services

The App uses AI services (Google Gemini, OpenAI) to parse voice input and automatically categorize todo items. Text you input is sent to these services for processing.

1.5 Device Information & Analytics

Third-party services we use (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) may collect non-identifiable device information, including:

  • Device model, operating system version
  • Advertising ID
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports

2. Use of Information

Information we collect is used solely to:

  • Provide and improve the App's core features (voice-powered task management)
  • Parse voice input via AI services
  • Sync your todos across devices
  • Display advertisements
  • Analyze App performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For user authentication, cloud data storage, and analytics. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: For serving advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: For managing subscriptions and in-app purchases. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini / OpenAI: For AI voice parsing. See their respective privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your todo data is primarily stored locally on your device. When cloud sync is enabled, data is encrypted in transit to Firebase Cloud Firestore, protected by security rules ensuring only you can access your own data. API keys are stored securely using the device's secure storage.

5. Data Retention

Your todos are retained until you choose to delete them. You can delete individual todos or sign out at any time. Uninstalling the App removes local data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access: You can view your todo data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual todos or clear all data
  • Opt-out of Ad Tracking: You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • Account Deletion: You can sign out and delete your account via the settings page

8. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be notified through App updates.
