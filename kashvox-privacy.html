KashVox Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 25, 2026

Overview

KashVox ("the App") is a personal finance and accounting application that helps users manage their income, expenses, and financial records. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection and Storage

All your financial data (transactions, accounts, categories, budgets) is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your financial data on any external servers.

Authentication

The App offers optional sign-in via Google or Apple accounts through Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we receive only your basic profile information (email address and display name) solely for account identification purposes. This information is managed by Firebase Authentication and is not shared with third parties.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostic data to improve app stability. This data does not contain any personally identifiable financial information.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication - For user sign-in (Google, Apple)
  • Firebase Crashlytics - For crash reporting and diagnostics
  • RevenueCat - For subscription management (if applicable)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by uninstalling the App. Since all financial data is stored locally, uninstalling the App will permanently remove all your records.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
