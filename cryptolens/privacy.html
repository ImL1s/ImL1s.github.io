Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CryptoLens ("we", "our", "the app") collects the following information when you use our service:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, display name, and Google account ID.
  • Chart Screenshots: When you submit charts for analysis, the images are processed by our AI engine. We do not permanently store the original images.
  • Analysis History: Your analysis results and metadata (symbol, timeframe, template type) are stored in your account.
  • Device Information: Firebase Cloud Messaging token for push notifications, device type, and platform.
  • Usage Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized usage data (screens viewed, features used, crash reports).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered chart analysis services
  • To maintain your analysis history and credits
  • To send push notifications about completed analyses
  • To improve our service through anonymized analytics
  • To display relevant advertisements (free tier users)

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore (data storage), Cloud Messaging (notifications), Analytics, Crashlytics
  • Google AdMob: Advertising for free tier users
  • Google Gemini AI: Chart analysis processing
  • Google Cloud Run: Backend API hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore with encryption at rest and in transit. We use Google OAuth 2.0 for authentication. We do not store your Google password.

5. Data Retention

Your analysis history is retained as long as your account is active. You can request data deletion by contacting us. Account deletion will remove all associated data within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

CryptoLens is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics collection

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes through the app or via email.
