Privacy Policy for FileBeam

Effective Date: March 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FileBeam. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding data deletion.

2. Information Collection and Use

FileBeam is a P2P file sharing application designed to connect devices on the same local network. The app does not collect, store, or transmit your files to any central servers. All file transfers occur securely between pairs of devices over the local network.

We use third-party services, such as Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics or AdMob, which may collect anonymous diagnostic and usage data to improve app stability and user experience.

3. Data Deletion Policy

Because FileBeam does not require an account and does not store personal data on our servers, there is no centralized data to delete. Any application data or settings reside locally on your device.

To delete all data associated with FileBeam, simply uninstall the application from your device. This will remove all local settings, transfer history, and any related application data.

If you have concerns regarding analytics data collected by third-party services, you can opt-out by uninstalling the app or contacting us.
